Hi, I'm Devon, and I'm based in Virginia. I earned my Bachelor's in Information Technology from American Public University (APU), and I'm now working toward a Master of Science in Cybersecurity Studies from APU.

This site is a running log of how that's going. I post updates in four tracks: studying for the CompTIA Security+ exam, working through TryHackMe rooms, reflecting on my Master's coursework, and keeping up with Spanish. The goal is a rough, honest timeline of my workflow, going through what I did, what I learned, and what I'd do differently, all for recruiters and potential employers to browse instead of a curated portfolio.

Away from the keyboard, I travel when I can, hike, cook, and read. Most recently I hiked in the Canadian Rockies in Alberta.
